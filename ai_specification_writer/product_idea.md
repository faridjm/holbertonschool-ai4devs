Product Idea: CargoFlow
Vision
CargoFlow is an internal logistics management platform for a moving and cargo transportation company. Its goal is to replace fragmented manual coordination with a centralized workflow for managing customer orders, vehicles, drivers, moving staff, payments, and operational performance. By combining clear business rules with AI-assisted recommendations, CargoFlow helps employees coordinate deliveries fairly and efficiently while keeping final decisions under human control.
Primary Users and Their Needs
- Operators / Dispatchers: Need to create and manage transportation orders, assign available vehicles, drivers, and moving staff, track order progress, and manage cancellations and payments.
- Drivers: Need to view their assigned orders and mark transportation jobs as completed when the work is finished.
- Company Manager: Needs visibility into orders, employee workload, vehicle utilization, payments, and operational performance to make informed decisions.
Moving staff are managed as company resources but do not log in to the platform.
Key Features
- Order Management: Create and track transportation orders from initial booking through completion or cancellation.
- Vehicle and Employee Assignment: Assign a vehicle and its designated driver, along with the required moving staff, while tracking availability and preventing overlapping active assignments.
- AI-Assisted Staff Recommendations: Recommend moving staff based on workload distribution and recent assignments, while allowing operators to override recommendations with a recorded reason.
- AI-Assisted Job Difficulty Assessment: Suggest an Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty level using factors such as cargo size and weight, item count, floor and elevator access, distance, and additional services. Operators review the suggestion before confirming it.
- Payment and Cancellation Tracking: Track advance and final payments and apply the company's defined cancellation and refund rules.
- Compensation and Bonus Tracking: Calculate moving staff bonuses based on completed assignments and applicable business rules.
- Management Reporting: Provide the manager with operational summaries and AI-assisted insights into workload, employee performance, and vehicle usage.
Key Differentiators
- Designed for Internal Operations: Focuses on the daily workflow of a small transportation company rather than a public delivery marketplace.
- Human-in-the-Loop AI: AI supports staffing and difficulty decisions, but operators remain responsible for final approval.
- Fairer Work Distribution: Recommendations consider recent assignments to reduce repeatedly selecting the same workers.
- Integrated Operational Rules: Vehicle availability, staff assignments, payment milestones, cancellations, and bonuses are handled within one coordinated workflow.
